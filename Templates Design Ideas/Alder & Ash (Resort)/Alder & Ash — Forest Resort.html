FAQ

Good to know before you book.

What's the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Within 7 days, one night is charged. Group bookings follow a separate agreement sent at booking.

Is Alder & Ash open year-round?

Yes, with a shorter activity list from January–March while some trails are snow-covered. The hot springs and Hearth Room run all year.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes — note it during booking or email us 48 hours ahead. Vegetarian, vegan, and most allergies are handled daily by the kitchen.

Are pets or children welcome?

Children are welcome in Family Cabins and the Lodge House. Well-behaved dogs are welcome in select cabins for a $35/night fee.

How do I plan a group retreat or reunion?

Use the Group tab in the booking window or reach the events team directly through the contact page — we'll send a custom itinerary and quote.

Is there wifi and phone signal?

Wifi is available at the lodge and can be switched on in-room on request. Cell signal is weak by design — most guests come to lose it.
